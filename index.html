Licensed Childcare at Austin Ave, Coquitlam.

Our Learning Environment

Indoor Play Studio

Our dedicated space is designed for messy play, storytelling, and early cognitive discovery.

Secure Outdoor Space

A fully fenced backyard area allows for daily fresh air and nature exploration.

Guidance & Growth

Our small-group setting (max 8 children) allows for personalized emotional support and co-regulation.

  • Positive Redirection
  • Emotional Co-Regulation
  • Problem-Solving Skills
  • Safe & Clear Boundaries

*Strictly adhering to BC Child Care Licensing Regulations.

Scent-Free Facility

Allergy Aware

100% nut-free environment.
