Our Holistic Care Plan

The Whole-Child Approach

We focus on physical, social, and cognitive development through balanced, age-appropriate activities.

  • Cognitive discovery: Literacy and STEM play.
  • Social milestones: Collaborative group play.
  • Physical health: Daily movement exercises.
  • Nutrition: Following Canada’s Food Guide.

Why Choose Us?

We combine the warmth of a home with the professional standards of a premier learning center.

  • Maximum 8-child capacity for personalized attention.
  • Licensed & inspected by Fraser Health.
  • Warm, nurturing home-based atmosphere.
  • Experienced professional supervision.

Guidance & Growth Policy

We view behavior as communication. Our policy focuses on positive guidance to help children develop emotional intelligence and self-control.

  • Positive Redirection
  • Emotional Co-Regulation
  • Problem-Solving Skills
  • Safe & Clear Boundaries

*Our guidance protocols strictly adhere to the BC Child Care Licensing Regulation regarding prohibited practices.

Safety & Health Standards

Scent-Free Zone

We use only eco-friendly, fragrance-free cleaners to support children with respiratory sensitivities.

Allergy-Aware Facility

We are a 100% nut-free facility with rigorous protocols to manage individual food sensitivities.

Safe Drop-off Zone

Our designated, low-traffic transition area ensures arrivals and departures are safe and stress-free.

Wellness Checks

To keep our small group healthy, we perform daily wellness screenings and follow strict illness protocols.
